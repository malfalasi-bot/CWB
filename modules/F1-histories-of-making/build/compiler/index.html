Compose the final Edo concept

Every version of the Edo and the floating world unit, taken apart into twelve design decisions. Pick one option per decision and the bar above says at once whether the combination can be built, what it costs, and where it breaks.

  1. Pass 1 · Blind. Options are shuffled and their versions hidden. Mark each Approve, Maybe or Reject on the idea, not the polish.
  2. Pass 2 · Decide. Versions return, each option is scored against v4 (+ better, S same, − worse), and you choose one per row. A reason is required for every Reject and every choice.
  3. Check and write up. The ledger explains every pair, the blueprint shows where problems sit on the page, and the brief writes itself.

Versions

Six versions of the unit and the Atlas hub, each with today’s verdict for its own path, plus three placeholder side branches. Posters show the same beats in every version.

Composer

Rows are decisions, cards are the options earlier versions used. The solid line is your path; the dashed line is v4. Badges on other cards say what swapping to them would do.

Feasibility

Why the verdict says what it says: every pair of chosen options, the house rules, the budgets, and a schematic of the page you have composed.

Brief
