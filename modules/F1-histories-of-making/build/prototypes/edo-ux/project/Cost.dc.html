In 1842 the state cut the price of an ordinary sheet by about what share?
{{verdict}}
An ordinary ten-colour sheet sold for about 24 mon; the cap of the eleventh month of 1842 set 16, with seven or eight colour impressions at most. The trade answered with fewer colours, more triptychs at 60–72, and warriors.
