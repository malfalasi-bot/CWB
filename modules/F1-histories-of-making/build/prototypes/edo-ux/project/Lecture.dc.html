How many Great Waves were printed?
Reading A · from the blocks' wear
Up to about 8,000 impressions, inferred from how far the key block degrades across the 111 located sheets, ordered in eight states: breaks in the cartouche border, behind the right-hand boat, near Fuji's summit; new blocks for the sea late in the run.
Reading B · the number is unknowable
No blocks survive, no publisher's ledger for the series survives, and the wear tells us order, not count. "Perhaps 8,000" rests on a rate of wear assumed from other series. What is documented is 111 located impressions, and that "the" print is an edition.

Closing · six one-tap questions, two shown
1 · The blue entered Japan through which port?
Nagasaki: Dejima was the one Dutch door, and Chinese ships used the same port. Yokohama did not open until 1859. The map lights the port.
2 · Who designed the all-blue fan of 1829?
Eisen. His fan is the earliest known all-blue sheet to sell at scale; the timeline marks 1829 and 1831 two years apart.
