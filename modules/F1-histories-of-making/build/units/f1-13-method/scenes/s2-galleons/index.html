HonestyThe Acapulco → Puebla leg is undated and drawn dashed. Potosí stands for "New Spain and Peru" silver. Both Pacific legs exceed the 70° window, so the globe turns with the glyph. "As much as a third" is one estimate, probable. The enslaved people on the same ships are a card beside the leg (shot 5), never a mark on it; no count is recorded.
