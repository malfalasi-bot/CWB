GROUPING · MOVEMENT

Mingei

民藝

A 1925 name for everyday crafts, made famous by named makers.

NAMED BY
Yanagi Sōetsu, with the potters Kawai Kanjirō and Hamada Shōji, in 1925: “folk crafts” or “common crafts”
SPAN AND PLACES
Japan, 1925 to today; collecting in Tōhoku, Kyūshū and Okinawa; Joseon crafts exhibited; Ainu and Taiwanese work shown
MEMBERSHIP
Documented: the founders and their association Claimed: the anonymous makers it collected
LEAVES OUT
The makers it championed never chose the label; Korean, Okinawan, Ainu and Taiwanese work was gathered into a Japanese category
USED IN F1.20 Movements and revivals · F1.8 East Asia · Compare, with Arts and Crafts
