# F1 Unit Specs — batch 1

2026-09-29 · @Mohammad AlFalasi

Eight unit specs are drafted on template v3 to the F1.17 standard: F1.1–F1.4, F1.14, F1.15, F1.16 and F1.18. Each unit has its own tab, with the spec table, the ten template-v3 sections, 3–8 open objects read at source, and a fact-check list for G3. Together they reach all nine world chapters, but unevenly: Europe appears in all eight, Australia and the Pacific in three, and the steppe only in F1.3, so batch 2 has to carry the steppe.

## What the protocol screen removed

The screen ran before any searching, and it took things out of four units. F1.15 dropped an object Owen Jones drew from, which is a preserved Māori head. F1.16 excluded minkisi, mallqui and huaca objects, katsina masks, restricted Aboriginal designs, Sámi drums, Ghost Dance garments, reliquaries and tabots. F1.4 excluded Okina masks. F1.2 carries a content note on the Congo ivory, since the trade rested on forced porterage. Tongan, Māori and ni-Vanuatu wording waits for a reader from each community before it is published.

## Before G3

- **Fact-check.** Every tab ends with its list: the R1 and R2 canon rows used, living people, community names, UNESCO claims and licences. The first two rounds show these rows err at 8–14% when drafted from memory.
- **Length.** The specs run 3,000–3,400 words against a 3,000 target, most of it URLs. The learner-facing copy is far shorter; the spec is the build brief.
- **Next batches.** Batch 2 is F1.19, F1.19a, F1.19b (if Q36 is agreed), F1.20–F1.22a, F1.23 and F1.24. Batch 3 is F1.25–F1.31, with F1.28a and F1.30a. The partner-voice packs follow batch 1 (Q41).
