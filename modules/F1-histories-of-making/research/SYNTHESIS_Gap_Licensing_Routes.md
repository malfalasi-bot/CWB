## Part 1: Regional Gap-Fill Summary

### PACIFIC & OCEANIA
**Primary gaps identified:**
- Stick charts (Smithsonian NMNH Anthropology: restricted; **solution:** Europeana CC0 Stockholm example, 1887 acquisition)
- Budj Bim trap images (Commons: landscape CC0 only; UNESCO: © photographer; **solution:** Gunditjmara corporation authority required—may not be open-access suitable)
- Madjedbebe ground-edge axes (Australian Museum CC BY 4.0 but limited geography; **flag:** Mirarr notice requires consultation on any image use)
- Hōkūleʻa (Commons CC BY-SA 3.0 and DVIDS public domain ✓)

**Open solutions found:**
- Barkcloth: Te Papa "No Known Copyright Restrictions" (1909–1911 Fiji purchase) ✓
- Lapita: CC BY papers + Commons CC BY-SA with excavation provenance ✓
- Ground-edge axes (NSW Central Coast variant): Australian Museum CC BY 4.0 ✓

**Flagged sensitivities:**
- Funerary (Teouma Lapita dish: exclude)
- Indigenous ownership (Mirarr, Gunditjmara, Aboriginal custodianship)
- Taonga restrictions (even "No Known Copyright Restrictions" marked items may be restricted by Te Papa/Auckland Museum policy)

**Cross-cutting access routes proven:**
- Smithsonian Open Access: CC0 filter unreliable; check NMNH records individually
- Smithsonian 3D: CC0 available, NMNH often absent
- Europeana: reusability=open filter returns PD Mark, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA ✓
- Te Papa & Auckland Museum: taonga restricted despite copyright marking
- Creative Australia protocols (2019): non-commercial only, current version on mirror only

## Part 2: Licensing Route Template (Alternative Sources)

### Pattern 1: Pre-1970 Museum Collections (UNESCO 1970 Cutoff)
**Institutions proven:**
- Cleveland Museum of Art (CC0, API available, covered all regions)
- Met Museum (CC0, API available)
- Dumbarton Oaks (CC0, fieldwork documentation)
- Australian Museum (CC BY 4.0)
- SLAM (public domain)

**Use case:** Archaeological objects with documented provenance before Nov 17, 1970.

---

### Pattern 2: Open-Access Academic Papers (CC BY / KOGL / Government License)
**Verified journals & publishers:**
- Cambridge Core: CC BY 4.0 open-access ✓ (Sintashta)
- Nature Communications / Scientific Reports: **check per-article** (now offer CC BY or CC BY-NC-ND author choice)
- Antiquity: check per-article (varies by publication date)
- MDPI: check per-article (varies by author choice)
- Brill: check per-article
- Japan Government Standard Terms v2.0: CC BY 4.0 equivalent ✓ (ColBase)

**Use case:** Excavation photos, diagrams, interpretation; includes funerary/burial context notes that clarify sensitivity.

---

### Pattern 3: National Museum APIs (CC0 / KOGL Type 1)
**Verified sources:**
- Rijksmuseum (CC0, data.rijksmuseum.nl, no key needed)
- National Palace Museum Taipei (CC0 1MP, CC BY 4.0 6MP)
- National Museum of Korea: KOGL Type 1 (glass-plate images from shipwreck recoveries)
- ColBase Japan: CC BY 4.0 via Japan Government Standard Terms

**Use case:** Decorative arts, ceramics, textiles, metalwork; pre-1970 or living artisan collections.

---

### Pattern 4: Commons Photographer Licences (CC BY-SA / CC0)
**Verified use cases:**
- Pazyryk carpet alternative (Commons CC BY 2.0 photographer, not the Hermitage object itself)
- Architectural/landscape photography (ger, felt-making workshops, Cahokia mounds)
- Living artisans (Jamdani weaver, Mingei workshops)

**Use case:** Context, technique, living tradition; not the historical object itself.

---

### Pattern 5: Europeana (PD Mark, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA)
**Verified:**
- Stick chart (Stockholm, CC0, 1887 acquisition)
- Catalan Atlas (PD-Art)

**Use case:** European heritage objects with cross-institutional holdings; reusability=open filter works.

---

### Pattern 6: Non-Commercial or Permission-Only (Workarounds)
**Items with no commercial-safe solution:**
- Hermitage Pazyryk (permission-required; alternative: Commons photographer licence for technique, felt-making context)
- V&A William Morris (CC BY-NC-SA; alternative: Cleveland or Commons for comparable Arts & Crafts pieces)
- UNESCO ICH videos (non-commercial; alternative: academic papers describing practice)
- Tokamachi textiles (non-commercial)
- Cairo Genizah (permission-required; alternative: academic papers on scribal practices)

**Strategic response:** Provide context via open-access papers, use photographer/technique documentation, or substitute with comparable open-access items from same tradition.

---

### Pattern 7: Indigenous Custodianship (Requires Consultation, Not Auto-Open)
**Flagged items:**
- Mirarr (Madjedbebe): consultation required even for CC BY 4.0 objects
- Gunditjmara (Budj Bim): corporation authority required; may not permit image use
- U'mista (Northwest Coast): items marked "sacred regalia"—do not reproduce
- Te Papa taonga: restricted despite "No Known Copyright Restrictions" marking
- NAGPRA (Native American): consultation required before use
- Sámi duodji & maker's rights: Sámi custodianship guidance from Sámi Parliament needed

**Strategic response:** Flag items for consultation panel; do not assume open-licence permits use. Include consultation status on card metadata.

## Part 3: Remaining Gaps (No Commercial-Safe Open Solution)

### Unsolved gaps:
1. **Budj Bim trap images** — UNESCO © photographer, Commons landscape only, Gunditjmara authority required
2. **Mingei named-maker images** — Hamada, Leach, Kawai copyright-blocked until 2037–60 internationally; anonymous collected work available but not attributed maker
3. **Northwest Coast sacred regalia** — U'mista designates as non-reproducible; Cleveland CC0 objects flag feast ownership & NAGPRA consultation
4. **Mound 72 Cahokia burial images** — Commons landscape available; burial remains excluded
5. **Madjedbebe Kimberley ground-edge axes** — Mirarr notice; only NSW Central Coast variant has clean CC BY 4.0

### Workarounds:
- **Budj Bim:** Use Commons landscape CC0 + academic paper diagrams of trap structures
- **Mingei named makers:** Use anonymous collected work (Hamada's collectors' pieces, Commons ger/felt photographers)
- **Northwest Coast:** Use Cleveland CC0 feast objects + academic papers on NAGPRA protocols
- **Cahokia:** Use Commons landscape + academic papers on mound archaeology
- **Madjedbebe Kimberley:** Flag as requiring Mirarr consultation; use NSW variant or alternative Australian axes
