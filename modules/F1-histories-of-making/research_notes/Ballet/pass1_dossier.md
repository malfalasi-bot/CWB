## Arguments

Who was earliest on pointe (Gosselin c. 1813–15 · Brugnoli 1823 · Taglioni 1832 as aesthetic); whether the Romantic tutu was Lami's (design not found; Lecomte 1827 a precursor); the 1913 "riot" (a later label, 1922 or 1924); Orientalism in *Schéhérazade*; caricature and its reworking (Final Bow for Yellowface); labour, injury and pensions (Paris 2019, 2023); Russianness after February 2022.

## What the sources leave out

Named makers (a handful: Michael Cripps "J", Madame Manya, Linnette Roe, Isabelle Homer, Katie Green, the "two English gentlemen on a farm in Essex"); Cuba, Japan, South Africa (filled in part by pass 2); women's wages and contracts in the nineteenth-century corps; where the satin, hessian and tulle come from.
