# Ballet — Pass 2 verification notes

Course: F1 Histories of Making. Subject: ballet as an art form.
Compiled 2026-10-20 from live WebSearch/WebFetch of institutional, primary and scholarly pages.

Confidence key
- **documented** — the date/number/attribution was seen on an institutional or primary page (company, museum, library, charity register, union, publisher) or on Britannica/Oxford-type reference.
- **probable** — reliable secondary sources agree, but no institutional page was reached.
- **contested** — sources disagree; both readings are given.
- **not found** — not located in this pass; do not use without further work.

Conventions: "earliest known", never "first", unless the source itself makes the "first" claim, in which case it is quoted and flagged. Russian premieres are given Old Style (O.S., Julian) / New Style (N.S., Gregorian); the 19th-century gap is 12 days.

Environment note: Wikimedia Commons, Gallica item pages, Smithsonian collections.si.edu, Rijksmuseum search and JSTOR could not be fetched directly from this environment (robots or proxy refusals). Where that blocked a verification it is recorded as "not found (unreachable)" rather than guessed. Rijksmuseum single-object pages and Met/Cleveland/Cooper Hewitt/Princeton record pages were reachable.

## What remains unverified

The main gaps are (1) stage machinery: no institutional page confirmed the Salle Le Peletier flying apparatus for *La Sylphide* (12 March 1832) or a first production for the Bibiena *scena per angolo* "c. 1700" — the 1711 treatise is the only hard date; (2) makers: Freed's Anello & Davide link, Hackney output figures and the makers'-mark system, and any ROH-named dyers, wig makers or scenic artists; (3) non-European dates: Eliana Pavlova's 1927 Shichirigahama school, PACT Ballet 1963/2000, the Dance Theatre of Harlem "1974" tights date, and the NNTT opening month; (4) labour: no ABT or NYCB management statement and no 2019–24 NYCB *dancers'* dispute (only the 2023 orchestra dispute and a 2025 dancers' agreement); (5) open-licence objects: Wikimedia Commons, Smithsonian collections.si.edu, the Met collection API and the Gallica HTML terms page were unreachable from this environment, so Commons licence templates and a CC0 NPG Pavlova photograph are unconfirmed, and the Gallica wording is taken from the notice printed in a Gallica PDF rather than the terms page; (6) image-rights statements for NGA Australia, Wadsworth Atheneum, Harvard Theatre Collection, BnF-Opéra and Dansmuseet were not found. Contested points to carry forward: the "first ballet" label for 1581; the Académie Royale de Danse's thirteenth member and dissolution; Benesh book year and Rudolf Benesh's death year; Karinska's NYCB shop date (1963/1964); Dulcie Howes's birth year; the year "riot" was first applied to the *Rite* (1922/1924); and Auguste Bert's death date (1937 / "before 1945" / 1944), which decides nothing for EU/UK public-domain status now but matters for how his photographs are credited.
