# Ecosystem review: findings and the changes made

20 October 2026. Four audits ran in parallel (`audit_repo.md`, `critique_content.md`, `best_practice.md`, `critique_skill.md`; 26,000 words, every claim with a file path, a figure or a URL). This page is the synthesis and the record of what was changed in response. Decisions that are the user's are marked.

## The verdict in one paragraph

The project has built the right truth machinery (a register, confidence words, open licences, a protocol, a lint that reads pages) and the wrong things around it. The register is unsigned, unverified at source and split across five stores; the canon's 4,666 rows carry no row-level source and 0 of 495 places have coordinates. Nothing learner-facing exists inside the syllabus: 37 of 38 specs say "To write", and the one built unit (Edo, the module's best work and close to shippable) appears in no spec. The content outside Edo speaks in the project's voice, with claim ids and policy sentences in the learner's seat. The design skill is 18,438 words, contradicts itself in 24 places, hard-codes Edo (its lanes, its glyphs, its colours) and says nothing about pedagogy, voice or when not to build. The evals measure vocabulary: two assertions are literally `lambda: True`, a page passed 13/13 while inventing two facts and making the priority claim the rules forbid, and the baseline was scored on different evals from the treatment. No human has signed anything.

## The twelve findings that matter, ranked

1. **No human gate.** Every publisher studied (Britannica, Grove, Smarthistory, GOV.UK, Khan Academy) has one; the EU's 2026 guidelines say agent use is never a delegation of responsibility. Here: zero sign-off lines, 21 decisions of 3 October unanswered, 13 held communities with no named reader. *Change:* a signed, dated register header; a colophon on every page that says "unreviewed" until a named person reads it; the human read as the gate in the workflow.
2. **Sources are not checked against claims.** 2026 measurements: agent-cited links resolve > 94 % and are relevant > 80 % while factual support is 39–77 %; 3–13 % of cited URLs never existed. Here: 97 of 799 register claims have no URL, 45 rest on Wikipedia or a blog with 28 marked "documented", 400 have no depth note. *Change:* spot-checks (link works · page is about this · page says this value) on a sample and on every documented record that drives a tick, recorded per record; a failed documented record blocks the freeze; source kinds and an independence test before "triangulated"; access dates and archive copies.
3. **Five claim stores.** claims_v0 (110), claims_specs_v1 (799), Edo content.json (105), F1.13 claims.yaml (94), audit claims.csv. *Change:* one store per unit under one id scheme (`atlas/data/register/units/<unit>.yaml`), `claims_specs_v1.csv` migrated into it, frozen with a tag and a changelog. *(The migration is a task; the rule is in force now.)*
4. **The canon has no row-level sources and no coordinates.** 0 of 4,666 rows cite a source id or URL; 7.8 % touched by any review; 101 rows still carry the wrong "Smithsonian (CC0)" wording; 100 of 174 "firsts" still open with "First"; 42,482 Pleiades rows downloaded and 0 joined. *Change:* a `source_id;source_url` column required on R1/R2 rows before the hub serves them; the Pleiades join; the two sweeps. *(Tasks, in the workflow's build order.)*
5. **The learner is not in the room.** No page states an objective; the narration says "we write", "we hold both"; 87 bracketed claim ids and 86 confidence fields in F1.13's 1,506 words; six identical content notes; guesses that leak their answers; build notes rendered in learner type. *Change:* a writing skill (`hom-writing`): objectives in the second person before the first beat and as a self-check at the end; apparatus one tap down; confidence shown only when not documented; every guess a commitment with answer-dependent feedback; the closing contribution as the unit's end; a learner view and an editor view rendered from the same source.
6. **The research schema had no slot for ideas.** Events, people, objects, counts, arguments, gaps: it found dates and holders and missed concepts, techniques, works, supply, institutions and bodies. *Change:* twelve kinds (`RESEARCH_SCHEMA.md`) in a research skill (`hom-research`), two passes, source tiers, verification at the holder, the brief template.
7. **The skill contradicts itself** in 24 places (confidence words both required and forbidden in captions; the two accents both banned and assigned; 1.2 vs 1–1.5 s per leg; 9 vs 12 ticks; 3–7 vs 4–6 shots; "no first" as a word ban the skill breaks 84 times). *Change:* the contradictions resolved in the toolbox references (see the changelog); the skill split into a constitution, a writing skill, a research skill, a design toolbox and a lint runner; the monolith retired to a router.
8. **The evals test vocabulary.** *Change:* eight new evals (readability judged on a human-anchored scale; accuracy against the register with a seeded trap; rights verified against holder fixtures; objective to page; leaving out; when not to build; human flow as experienced; prose and vocabulary), three seeds each, same access for both arms, runner-captured timing. Defined in `skills/EVALS.md`; the harness fix is the first migration task.
9. **Licences in the builds are not what the rules say.** Edo embeds 29 Commons images tagged only "Public domain" with no verification field, including British Museum and MFA photographs the licence file marks link-only; 25 open objects "to check"; allowed-licence sets differ across four files. *Change:* `rights_uri` and `rights_checked` on every object record; the lint refuses an image without them; the constitution names the one allowed set.
10. **Method resets without reconciliation.** Four in three weeks, the last contradicting the same day's SKILL.md; the F1.13 run claimed no spec existed while `specs/F1.13.md` (40 KB) did, because the method pointed at the wrong folder. *Change:* `WORKFLOW.md` is the one current description; `specs/F1.N.md` is the source of truth for a unit; a build updates the spec rows it fulfils; every reset is a changelog entry that says what it replaces.
11. **No reader-facing history, corrections route or AI statement.** *Change:* a per-unit `CHANGELOG.md`, "originally published / last revised" on every page, the colophon with which model drafted, which checks ran, what a human read.
12. **Coverage is overstated.** `coverage.py` counts open-ended rows to 2100 (about 2.3×) and labels harvester candidates "verified"; the README says 35 units and 4,228 nodes against 38 and 4,666. *Change:* counts generated by script; "candidates" labelled as such. *(Task.)*

## What is working, and stays

The register idea, the confidence vocabulary, the protocol, the human-flow rule, the licence discipline in intent, the lint. The paper finish. The 20 October research passes' habit of saying "not found".

## Decisions for the user

- **Build order.** The audit's recommendation: three syllabus units to a readable page before any more framework (F1.13 with its images fetched; F1.25; F1.8 with Edo folded in as its case), and pause the ballet, compiler and study-page lines until then. The ballet brief stands ready as the writing skill's first instance; it can be the fourth, or the first if you prefer a subject the canon does not yet hold.
- **The human reader.** Who signs the register, and who reads the held communities; nothing on the Atlantic or the Indian Ocean's forced movements publishes without the named reader.
- **The 21 decisions of 3 October** (`DECISIONS_2026-10-03.md`), still unanswered.
