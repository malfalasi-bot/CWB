## Identity (`identities.md`)

F1.13 is a cross-world unit with one lecture of nine stations, not eight lectures; the identity is one colour and one motif for the lecture, derived from a material row the unit reaches. **Cobalt** (the jar's blue, MAT row "cobalt" via NET054) is the unit's material; its muted reading, kept ≥ 36° from Edo's set and off the two accents, is `#4A6472` (a cobalt-grey, 6.1:1 on paper). Motif: the jar (a content line demands it: "one jar's blue, followed through all four"), added to the shared glyph library as `jar`. The colour appears on the lecture's card, its threshold, its tick, its panel header and the journey bar's travelled part; never on a route, a mark or a wash. Provisional until the unit's specialist confirms; hue spacing against the eight other world chapters cannot be checked until they derive theirs.
