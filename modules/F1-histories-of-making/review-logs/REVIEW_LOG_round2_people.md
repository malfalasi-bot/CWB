## Scope and results

- Priority set (canon_people): 124 rows = every R1/R2 row, every medium/low-confidence row and every living-community row (includes all 14 flagged items). Rows with substantive errors: 16 (PER027, PER032, PER059, PER067, PER073, PER087, PER120, PER180, PER236, PER264, PER303, PER331, PER339, PER380, PER466, PER476), about 13%. Notes-only verification updates: PER006, PER068, PER274, PER296, PER340, PER463.
- Random sample, 50 further people rows (seed 20260929; ids in scratchpad/r2_sample_people.txt): 1 row wrong (PER320 Şahkulu: "Tabriz-born" and an invented 1500 start date), 2%.
- Random sample, 30 missing_entities rows (seed 20260929; ids in scratchpad/r2_sample_missing.txt): 0 errors found; missing_entities.csv not changed.
- Checked and left unchanged: Leizu (PER159) and Lu Ban (PER160), both framed as legend and consistent with sources; Amina's wall attribution; Safeya Binzagr 1940–2024; Whang-od still living (turned 109 in Feb 2026); Esther Mahlangu, Mary Lee Bendolph and other living people's birth years.
- Error types: two people listed as living had died (Friedeberg 2026, Graburn 2025); unsourced or wrong birth years for living people (softened to a floruit where they could not be sourced); unsupported biographical claims (Sethna, Makki, Sahifa Banu "copied", Kwali "New York", Fry "British-born", Şahkulu "Tabriz-born"); a wrong personal name (al-ʿIjliyya, "Mariam" is modern); wrong date range (Aj Maxam); a variant spelling of a Haida name.
- Not changed, but a verifier may want to look again: PER392 Dürer's complaint to Venice about Marcantonio rests mainly on Vasari's account; PER108 note on Maria Martinez signing other potters' pots is still marked "verify".
