# Histories of making: start here

1. `hom-constitution`: the twelve invariants. Always.
2. `hom-research`: two passes, twelve kinds, the register, the benchmark, the brief. Before a unit is designed.
3. `hom-writing`: the learner, objectives, reading orders, the paragraph, captions, what to leave out, when not to build. Before any HTML.
4. `hom-design-toolbox`: tokens, components, scenes, the lane chart, identities, scripts, calendars, the checklist. One reference per page.
5. `hom-lint`: the claims lint and the prose lint. Before done.

The module's workflow is `WORKFLOW.md` at the module root; the record of rule changes is `CHANGELOG.md`.
