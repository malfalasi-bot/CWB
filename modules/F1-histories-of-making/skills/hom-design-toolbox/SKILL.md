# The design toolbox

Available, not required. The constitution (`hom-constitution`) says what must be true; the writing skill (`hom-writing`) decides what the page says and in what order; this toolbox dresses it. Load one reference at a time.

## Defaults (change them when the subject asks; say why in the editor view)

Paper `#F1EADB` with the grain, ink `#1D1A15`, two accents only (indigo for the trade, seal red for the state and the cursor); Source Serif 4 at 17/1.55 (16 on phones), lines ≤ 68 characters; IBM Plex Sans for labels, IBM Plex Mono for readouts; titles pair roman and italic; one large thing per page; captions under, never over; the credit line in mono; rules, boxes and shadows only over maps and images; works at 390 px with no horizontal scroll; `tokens.css` copied as-is (its Edo block is an example of a unit's own identity tokens).

## Three refusals the toolbox makes itself

A listable set of places is a list, not a globe. A journey with no dated legs is a bar with the line that says so, not a scene. An interactive that reveals what a paragraph could hold is cut. (hom-constitution rule 11; hom-writing §8.)

## Where the contradictions were resolved (20 October 2026)

A caption may carry a probable, contested or argued value only with its word in the sentence (the earlier "no confidence word in captions" rule is withdrawn); the accents are lecture colours in Edo's L5 and L6 only, and the exception is written where the rule is; a page without a date shows no clock; 1.2 s per leg, 2.5–4 s per tick, ≤ 9 ticks per focus, 3–7 shots per scene; "no first" is a ban on priority claims, not on the word; the scene schema carries `content_note` and `clock.claim`; the highlight vocabulary has seven kinds; "68 characters" is the line measure. The `CHANGELOG.md` at the module root records each.
