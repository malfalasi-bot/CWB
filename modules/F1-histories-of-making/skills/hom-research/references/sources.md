# Source tiers and independence

Every source in `sources.yaml` carries: `id`, `title`, `url`, `date_accessed`, `archive_url` (a Wayback save made at access), `last_checked`, `kind`, `interest`.

| Kind | Examples | What it can support |
|---|---|---|
| holder record | a museum's collection page or API; a library catalogue; an archive finding aid | an object's holder, accession, date, materials, maker, licence: documented |
| primary | a treatise, a letter, a receipt, a decree, a newspaper of the day, a photograph with its negative record | an event, a date, a quotation: documented |
| peer-reviewed | a journal paper, a monograph from a university press | a finding, a rate, a count with its method: documented |
| reference work | Grove, the Oxford Dictionary of Dance, ODNB, PeriodO, TGN | a date, a definition, a period as an assertion: documented when the entry is signed and dated |
| institutional | UNESCO, a company's history page, a ministry, a theatre's own page | its own acts and dates: documented; its claims about itself: labelled as its own |
| press | a newspaper or magazine report, a journalist's visit | an event, a named person's words: probable; a count: labelled as reported |
| teaching page | a museum blog, a course page, a video | a lead and a presentation model; probable at best |
| community statement | a community's own site, a Local Contexts Hub entry, a signed review | the community's name, protocol and narrative: documented for what it is |
| aggregator | Wikipedia, Britannica, Baidu Baike, a fan site | a lead; a row that rests only on one says so and stays probable |

Independence: two sources are independent when neither copies the other and they do not share an author, publisher or dataset; a syndicated copy, a press release reprinted, or two pages citing one catalogue are one source. "Documented" needs one of the first four kinds, or two independent sources of any kind above aggregator.

Interest: a company, a fundraiser, a government, an institution describing itself, or a party to a dispute is an interested source; its number is taught with its name on it ("the company's figure"), never as a bare fact.

Calibration: after each human spot-check, record per confidence word how many records held; a wrong "documented" counts worse than a gap.
