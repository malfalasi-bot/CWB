## Corrections found in the canon and registers

- **GAP072:** the Chambre Syndicale de la Couture Parisienne dates from 14 December 1910.
- **GAP073:** Le Bon Marché was founded in 1838. 1852 is when Boucicaut became a partner.
- **OBT042:** kanga demand grew by the 1860s, so the start date of 1880 is too late.
- **STY122:** the Kalighat painting is gum tempera, not watercolour.
- **OCC217:** Utility furniture was designed from 1941, and its first catalogue appeared in 1943.
- **OCC141:** its claim that the Long Walk led weavers to commercial yarn was not found in any source opened. Drop it or source it.
- **OCC175:** "punitive expedition" is the perpetrators' term. Put it in quotation marks, or rename the row.
- **DYN005, MKR090:** "first recorded strike" should read "earliest recorded".
- **OCC055:** "deportation" against the source's "brought": give both readings.
- **MOV096:** Mingei dates to 1925 (museum) or 1926 (canon).
- **MKR047:** ends at 1850, although jamdani weaving continues.
- **WS-100:** the Pazyryk carpet is named in Deep Dives 2 but missing from `world_set_v0.csv`.
