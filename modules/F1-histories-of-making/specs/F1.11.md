## F1.11 Europe and the Mediterranean, read through the guild

Europe is one world of making among several, and its guild rules show who was let in to make and who was kept out.

| Opening move | **Object first (form 10).** Rembrandt's *Syndics* (Rijksmuseum SK-C-6, 1662): men in black around a table, an open book, a carpet.
