## F1.21 Consumer society, the digital and the platform

Every age of buying has a counter that sets the terms between maker and buyer, from the Edo cloth shop to the platform, and the maker's name, script and share are decided there.

**2. Protocol screen**

- No human remains, sacred or secret material, grave goods or altar objects in any case.
- **Kalighat:** many Kalighat pictures show deities; they were market goods, not altar objects. The unit shows a secular scene (Cleveland 2003.107.a).
- **Kanga:** a living practice of Swahili-speaking communities (COM061 Waswahili), with a Notice. Sayings are quoted only from published sources, with the community's word (jina) first. No open image; link out.
- **Uncle Josh:** the printed monologue has no ethnic caricature (claim 9), but the recording may differ. It is heard in full and transcribed before use.
- **Platforms:** no real seller, shop or listing is shown; the video uses an invented shop.
- **People never goods:** the cotton behind Paris cloth and the kanga was grown by coerced and colonised workers. One line names them as people, linking F1.13 and F1.23.

**9. Readings and risks**

- **Historian pre-read (pack v2) should check:** the earliest-store framing (claim 4) and whether the Mitsui archive is quoted fairly. Worth's "earliest label" (claim 6) needs a fashion-history source. The kanga origin (claim 11) rests on family and trade accounts. Simplified Arabic (claim 13) must be read in Nemeth 2017; the Reading repository refused access. Spellings: Surugachō, *genkin kakene nashi*, jina, Anandabazar Patrika.
- **Balance:** Paris and New York must not read as the centre. Edo opens the scroll. Non-Latin scripts are shown as re-engineered to fit machines made for Latin, not as problems.
- **Hold until a reader clears:** a full listen of Victor 665 against the print. The AI example: live litigation; Getty's appeal was reported granted in January 2026 (Taylor Wessing; page refused, not read). This spec was drafted with an AI system, so a human should check the AI readings for balance. The Etsy rule is dated 2013, not current policy.
