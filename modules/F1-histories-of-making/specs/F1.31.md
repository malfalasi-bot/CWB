## F1.31 Regional slot: the learner's own place, authored locally

The program builds the frame for a place's history of making, and the people who hold that history write it; this unit specifies the frame, the authorship rules and the review, and leaves the content empty.

**What the slot is, and who may author it**

A regional slot is an empty, structured Atlas layer for one place. It holds the place's own account of its making, with sources, consent and review recorded. In the Lineage view it is a place layer: it groups the author's nodes, draws typed edges (made in, learned from, traded with, named by) to canon rows, and can take its time axis from the Calendars tool (F1.30).

| Tier | Who may author | Who sees it | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Learner slot | Any learner, for their own place | The learner and partner voices | Minimums met or gaps named |
| 2. Shared slot | The learner, with co-authors they name | Peers they choose | Consultation record filled for any living practice; Cultural note on Indigenous material; marked "not reviewed" |
| 3. Published regional slot | Local authors only: holders, community members, or people the holders name; credited | Every learner | Full review; credit and benefit recorded; withdrawal route live |

**The program does not define "local".** The slot records the author's stated relation and the holders' endorsement. Program staff and AI systems never write tier-3 content (staff who are local authors by this rule excepted). Global units are never translated into a slot (finding 2.5).

**Template minimums.** (1) One holders' own account: community-published, a cooperative's text, or a consented oral account from the F1.29 kit, with recordings kept on the learner's device. (2) One living practice as a Practice record, dated to its source. (3) One outside record (state, museum, travel or colonial), labelled by position. (4) One earlier node linked into canon, or a stated gap. (5) One open object, or why none passes. (6) A Source Grader result (F1.28) on every source. (7) The consultation record: who, when, what was agreed, credit, what was given back. (8) A gaps register, with "who the record leaves out" and labour (F1.23).

**Review path (tier 3).** A program editor checks form, licences and protocol only. Holders sign off all practice content. A human reader from the place reads it (Phase 4, item 15, option B). The Historian pre-reads, logged, never sufficient. Reviews are dated and repeated every two years, or at once when holders ask. Withdrawal requests are acted on within 48 hours.
