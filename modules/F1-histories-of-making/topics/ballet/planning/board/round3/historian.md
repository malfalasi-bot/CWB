## Remaining issues

**1. (Medium) The chronology paragraph has not moved since v1 except for its hedges.**
> "Amalia Brugnoli rose onto the tips of her toes on a Vienna stage in 1823, in soft slippers held by strength alone; whether she was the earliest is contested."
> "The block that made it safer probably came half a century later, from Italy."

Still *Pointe Magazine* and Wikipedia; still no rival named for a "contested" priority (Gosselin c. 1815; the 1821 Waldeck lithograph of Fanny Bias); still "from Italy" with no Italian virtuose, no Russia, no Pavlova, although pass2_body has Pavlova's reinforced box. "Vienna" has no source in the register at all (the editor note says it came from an ENB page in pass 1 that is not listed). The coordinator records Hammond and Guest as open; until they are read, this paragraph is the page's one section a specialist would not accept.
*Fix for now:* add the ENB page as a source for Vienna or drop the word; add one clause naming Gosselin and Bias so "contested" has content; add a "Further reading" line under the section: Hammond, "Searching for the Sylph", *DRJ* 19/2 (1987–88); Guest, *The Romantic Ballet in Paris*; Barringer and Schlesinger, *The Pointe Book*. That costs twenty words and turns a hedge into a pointer.

**2. (Low) "One firm has sold a moulded plastic shoe since 1993" is still twice wrong.**
The Gaynor Minden shoe is satin, suede and foam with a moulded elastomer shank and box; it is not "a plastic shoe", and other firms have since sold thermoplastic shanks (Bloch TMT). Flagged in rounds 1 and 2.
*Fix:* "one firm has sold a shoe with a moulded plastic shank and box since 1993, and others have followed".

**3. (Low) Two negatives are still stated as if the page had surveyed the field.**
> "the nearest count of this labour that exists"
> "The one number missing from every account is what a maker is paid for a pair."

Both should be scoped to what was read: "the nearest count this page found"; "missing from every account read for this page". The page already uses that form in "What is not here"; use it here.

**4. (Low) Heritage Crafts: "two businesses in the country" overstates the listing.**
The page says two businesses *employing two or more makers* (Freed and Suffolk), with 21–50 professionals; sole makers are not counted out.
*Fix:* "two businesses with more than one maker".
